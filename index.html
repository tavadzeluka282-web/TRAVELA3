When to go

Every season has its own Georgia

Spring

Green hills, blossom in the valleys and mild days in Tbilisi. A good time for cities and culture.

Summer

Beach days in Batumi and cool air in the highlands, when Svaneti and Kazbegi trails are open.

Autumn

Harvest season in Kakheti, with grape picking and new wine. Warm days and gold light.

Winter

Ski at Gudauri, soak in Tbilisi baths and see the mountains under snow.

Getting to Georgia

Land in the capital, the west or on the coast

Georgia has three international airports, so you can start your trip close to where you want to stay.

Tbilisi

The main gateway, close to the capital and the road to Kazbegi and Kakheti.

Kutaisi

The way into Imereti, with onward routes to Svaneti and the Black Sea coast.

Batumi

Straight to the seaside, for beach days and a stay on the boulevard.
